# NovaMart Sales & Revenue Analytics

## Project Overview

NovaMart Sales & Revenue Analytics is a Power BI business intelligence project developed to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, customer behavior, product performance, promotions, and returns.

The project uses a fictional retail business dataset containing sales transactions and related customer, product, store, payment, promotion, and return information.

The dashboard is designed to transform raw business data into interactive visualizations and actionable analytical insights.

---

## Business Questions

This project focuses on answering key business questions such as:

- How are sales and revenue performing over time?
- Which products, categories, and brands generate the most revenue?
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue and orders?
- How does customer purchasing behavior vary?
- What are the major return reasons and affected products?
- How are promotions and discounts performing?
- How do returns affect overall business performance?

## Dataset

The project uses a fictional retail business dataset containing multiple related tables:

- Customers
- Products
- Sales
- Returns
- Stores
- Payments
- Promotions
- Date

The dataset contains approximately 50,000 sales records along with supporting customer, product, store, payment, promotion, and return data.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of the business, including:

- Gross Revenue
- Net Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Products
- Total Quantity Sold
- Return Quantity
- Transaction Returns
- Store performance
- Revenue trends
- Category-level revenue

### 2. Sales & Time Analysis

Analyzes sales and revenue performance over time using:

- Monthly revenue trends
- Gross and net revenue
- Revenue growth
- Returns rate
- Quantity sold
- Return quantity
- Year and month comparisons

### 3. Product & Category Analysis

Analyzes product-level and category-level performance using:

- Top products by gross revenue
- Top products by quantity sold
- Revenue by brand
- Revenue by subcategory
- Average selling price
- Average product cost
- Average discount
- Product margin
- Price vs quantity analysis

### 4. Customer Analysis

Examines customer behavior and contribution through:

- Customer segments
- Customers by country
- Top customers by revenue
- Revenue by customer segment
- Orders by customer segment
- Customer revenue vs order frequency
- Average customer age

### 5. Promotion & Returns Analysis

Analyzes promotional activity and product returns through:

- Promotion types
- Average discount by promotion type
- Total promotions
- Return rate
- Monthly return quantity
- Return reasons
- Returns by store
- Returns by product
